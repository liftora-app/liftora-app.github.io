How Liftora handles your data, and how to reach us

Liftora is a small workout-tracking app in beta testing. This page is the whole story: who runs it, what it stores, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

Support

Liftora is run by Lars Sarmiento, Oslo, Norway.

Email: larssarmiento@gmail.com

Write to that address for anything: a bug, a question about your data, a request to delete something, or to report a person or a post. The operator promises to read and act on reports of abuse within 24 hours. Everything else gets an answer within a few days.

How to delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account, then enter your password to confirm. This removes everything about you from Liftora in one step (see Deleting below). You do not need to email us, but you can if the app is not working for you, and we will delete the account by hand.

How to report someone or something

On a post or a profile, tap … and choose Report post or Report account; on a comment, press and hold it and choose Report comment. Then pick a reason. You can also block an account from its profile; blocked accounts cannot follow you or see your workouts, and the app never tells them. If the app's own buttons are not enough, email us with a description of what you saw and, if you have it, the person's friend code or display name.

Testing through TestFlight

During the beta you install Liftora through Apple's TestFlight. Apple collects crash logs and usage information from TestFlight testers and shares them with us; if you were invited by email, that includes your name and email address. We use this only to fix the app. Apple's own description of what TestFlight shares is at apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight. To see or delete the TestFlight data we hold about you, email us.

Where it is stored

With Supabase, on servers in Stockholm, Sweden (EU). Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) runs the service for us as a processor under its data-processing addendum, hosted on Amazon Web Services. Supabase keeps its service logs in EU data centres. Two things can leave the EU: your profile picture and workout photos may pass through Supabase's content delivery network when they are shown, and Supabase support staff in Singapore or the United States may access the service under the EU's standard contractual clauses. Supabase's list of sub-processors is at supabase.com/legal/subprocessors.

Location, camera and photos

Liftora reads your location only when you tap the gym field after a workout, to suggest gyms near you. Your position is sent to Supabase at full precision for that search and is not stored, unless you add a new gym, in which case it is stored rounded to three decimal places as the gym's position. The camera opens when you set a profile picture, add a photo to a workout or scan a friend's code; the photo picker opens only when you set a profile picture or add a photo to a workout. Liftora receives only the picture you take or choose, never the rest of your photo library. A scanned code is sent to Supabase only to find that person, exactly as a typed code is. It is not saved in Liftora's database; like every request, it can appear for a short time in Supabase's service logs (see How long data is kept). No background location, no motion tracking, no contacts, no microphone.

On your phone

Your sign-in token, cached copies of the pictures and photos shown to you, your theme choice, your weight unit, a workout in progress, a note left when the app saves or discards a workout you left open (whether it was saved or discarded, when you last worked on it, which workout if it was saved and whether it was already posted, and your account's id), and a cached exercise list.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Everything above is kept for as long as your account exists, except that attempt-log entries are deleted within two days, and a photo or picture you remove or replace is deleted at once; if that fails, the app deletes it later, on a later day when you next use Liftora. The operator promises to delete closed reports by hand once a month, once they were filed more than 90 days earlier; until then the database keeps them. Delete account in Settings (enter your password to confirm) removes it all in one step: your workouts, posts and the photos on them, templates, favourites, exercise notes, the gyms you added, your picture, and your login. It also removes your profile card, your follow requests and follows in both directions, blocks you made and blocks others made of you, the heias and comments you gave and the ones others left on your posts, the reports you filed and the reports others filed about you, your attempt log, and your notification record. The picture and photo cache, your saved unit and the note about a workout the app closed for you are cleared on the phone you delete from; your theme choice and the cached exercise list are not about you and stay. You can also email us to have the account deleted.

Five things Delete account does not reach:

  • Supabase's service logs, which record your email address, your phone's internet address, a rough location derived from that address, a description of your device, and when you signed in and used the app. They can also hold the address of each request, which can include a name or friend code you searched. On our current plan Supabase keeps most of these for one day and the sign-in audit log for one hour.
  • Backups. Liftora keeps no backups of its own; data you delete is gone at once and cannot be recovered by us either.
  • Your friend code is retired, not deleted: it stays in the database linked to no account, nobody can look it up, and it is never given to anyone else.
  • Copies of your workouts that other members made with Copy split: their template keeps the workout's title, exercises, set counts and the name you had at that time, as text, with no link to your account.
  • Email you sent us. What you wrote to larssarmiento@gmail.com, and our replies, stay in the operator's Gmail account until the operator deletes them. The operator deletes a conversation once the matter is closed, or at once if you ask.

Your rights

You can see your data in the app, change it under Edit profile, and delete it with Delete account. For a copy of your data in a file, a correction we cannot make in the app, or any question, email larssarmiento@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or EEA you also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority.
